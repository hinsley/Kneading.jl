# ZILES — zoomed-in Lyapunov exponent sweep

ZILESmax denotes the approved **maximum**-exponent plot, with the tightened
margins, enlarged LaTeX labels, finite colorbar ticks, and increased tick-label padding.

- `ZILESmax.png`: preserved approved maximum plot.
- `render_ZILES_max.jl`: reproduction script for that plot.
- `ZILESmin.png`: matching minimum-exponent variant.
- `render_ZILES_min.jl`: reproduction script for the minimum variant.
- `lyapunov_3000.jld2`: original Float64 exponents for BOTH critical orbits,
  their maximum, the 3000 × 3000 parameter coordinates, and computation settings.
- `contours_3000.jld2`: all 76 original contour layers.
- `compute.jl`: RTX 4070 Ti computation and validation.
- `Project.toml`, `Manifest.toml`, and `Kneading.jl/`: reproducible Julia environment.
- `README.md` and `validation.txt`: methodology and numerical checks.

Run from this directory:

```sh
julia --startup-file=no --threads=4 --project=. render_ZILES_max.jl
julia --startup-file=no --threads=4 --project=. render_ZILES_min.jl
```

The minimum version uses elementwise min(lambda_left, lambda_right), computed
from the same saved orbit estimates. Only that aggregation and the colorbar
label change; contour geometry, styling, color mapping, ticks, and layout are
identical. No new orbit scan is required.

ZILESmin is the version selected for the paper; ZILESmax is retained only as an archive.

## Log-coordinate exploration (separate from the paper)

`ZILESmin_log.png` uses natural-log coordinates x = log(u), y = -log(-v),
with u in [0.01, 1] and v in [-1, -0.01]. A fresh 3000 × 3000 scan is
uniform in x and y; contour interpolation is also performed in those coordinates.
The minimum exponent, orbit lengths, color mapping, and contour styling match ZILESmin.

- `compute_ZILESmin_log.jl`: computes `lyapunov_log_3000.jld2` and `validation_log.txt`.
- `render_ZILESmin_log.jl`: caches `contours_log_3000.jld2` and renders the image.
- `preview_ZILESmin_log.png`: smaller preview.

Run both scripts with `julia --startup-file=no --threads=4 --project=.` as above.

## Corrected logarithmic exploration: emphasize u = 1 and v = -1

`ZILESmin_boundary_log.png` supersedes the origin-emphasizing log exploration.
The plotted coordinates are x = -log(1-u), y = log(1+v), sampled uniformly
on a fresh 3000 × 3000 grid. The domain is u in [0, 0.99], v in [-0.99, 0].
Axes and ticks show the original u and v values, not the transformed coordinates.

- `compute_ZILESmin_boundary_log.jl`
- `render_ZILESmin_boundary_log.jl`
- `lyapunov_boundary_log_3000.jld2`
- `contours_boundary_log_3000.jld2`
- `validation_boundary_log.txt`

The paper's ZILESmin remains the original linear-axis version.

The boundary-log renderer includes `cairo_bounded_segments.jl`, which bounds temporary projection buffers. Its output was verified pixel-for-pixel against standard CairoMakie on a small grid. Full-resolution output: 3000 × 2373 pixels, from 3000 × 3000 samples and 140,049,426 contour segments.

## Refined boundary-log styling

`ZILESmin_boundary_log_refined.png` and `render_ZILESmin_boundary_log_refined.jl`
reuse the full boundary-log data and contours. Ticks show 0, 0.5, 0.8, 0.9,
0.95, 0.98, 0.99 on u and their negatives on v. Contour opacity is n^(-1.5)
instead of n^(-1.2), with n = iterate - 1; colors and line widths are unchanged.
The original boundary-log image remains available for comparison.

## Tapered contours with slower opacity decay

`ZILESmin_boundary_log_tapered.png`, generated by
`render_ZILESmin_boundary_log_tapered.jl`, uses linewidth 2*n^(-0.5)
and opacity n^(-1.2), where n = iterate - 1. The first contours keep linewidth
2 and full opacity; the last (n=19) have linewidth approximately 0.459 and
opacity approximately 0.0292. Tick labels, coordinates, data and colors match
the refined boundary-log version.

## Slower opacity falloff

`ZILESmin_boundary_log_tapered_opacity09.png` and its matching `render_ZILESmin_boundary_log_tapered_opacity09.jl` use opacity n^(-0.9), retaining linewidth 2*n^(-0.5) and all other settings from the tapered version.
